Privacy Policy

Help Queue · Last updated August 2026

What we collect

When a student joins a room's queue, the app stores:

  • The name they typed (not verified — it's whatever they enter)
  • The problem description they typed
  • An automatic timestamp of when they joined
  • An automatic timestamp of when a teacher marks them "Helped" (if applicable)

No accounts, emails, or logins are required, and none are collected.

What it's used for

Solely to display a live, ordered help queue to the teacher and students viewing that room while it's open. That's it — this data isn't used for grading, analytics, advertising, or any other purpose.

Who can see it

Anyone with a room's link can view that room's live queue while it's open — that's the intended use, the same way a name written on a classroom whiteboard would be visible to the class. A 4-digit PIN, known only to the teacher who created the room, is required to manage the queue (mark students helped, reorder, remove entries, end or delete the room, or export a CSV) through the app's interface.

How long it's kept

Every room and everything in it is automatically and permanently deleted 24 hours after the room is created. This isn't optional or something a teacher has to remember to do — it's enforced by the hosting database's built-in expiration feature. If a teacher downloads a CSV before that window closes, that exported file is theirs to keep or delete as they choose — this policy only covers data inside the app itself.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database, only for the 24-hour lifespan of a room. It is not shared with, or sold to, any third party.

Questions

Contact the teacher who set up this tool with any questions about how it's used in your classroom.
